# Meet AI Networking Admin Coach

# 1\. Project Overview

My Al Career Coach is designed to help a prospecting networking professional with a career pivot from general IT to networking.

* Career pathway: Networking  
* Built with: Google NotebookLM

# 2\. The Problem I'm Solving

What career challenge inspired this assistant?  
Why would someone benefit from this tool?

As a recent computer science graduate that is interested in computer networking, navigating the current technology market has posed a challenge. Many technical tools, skills, and experiences that are expected of a Networking Administrator were not a primary focus of my academic journey, making it so that gaining this knowledge required initiative from myself. 

I needed to learn on my own how to navigate understanding my qualifications for the position I desire, interview preparedness, and an action plan to make myself a stronger candidate. My solution to this problem is to create an AI Coaching assistant that can help me navigate this challenge.

Anyone in a position where they are trying to enter a new field, but lack professional experience or a clear roadmap to achieving this goal can benefit from an AI Coach. AI coaches can pull from a variety of sources, and they can aid in role playing as a specialised recruiter, interviewer, or even pep talker.

# 3\. How My Al Career Coach Works

I curated resources including:

* A Jr Networking Administrator job description for retail company called Boot Barn  
* My IT focused resume that showcases my experience.  
* Documentation that highlights a home lab project to showcase my unique approach to problem solving

I used NotebookLM to analyze these resources and provide source-grounded career guidance.

# 4\. My Al Career Coach in Action

## Example 1: Career Guidance

### My prompt 

“\[SYSTEM / ROLE\] Act as an expert Technical Recruiter and Hiring Manager specializing in IT and Network Infrastructure. Your goal is to evaluate candidate fit, identify skill gaps, and provide actionable resume refactoring strategies. \[CONTEXT\] The candidate is a CS graduate pivoting from IT internship and general Systems Support into a specialized networking role. You must evaluate work history, academic background, and hands-on home labs/projects.   
\[DYNAMIC VARIABLES\] \- Candidate Background: resume included in sources \- Hands-On Projects / Labs: home lab included in my sources \- Target Job Description: Included in source   
\[OUTPUT CONSTRAINTS & STRUCTURE\] Generate the evaluation using the following four sections only:   
1\. FIT ASSESSMENT & MATCH SCORE (0-100%) \- High-level verdict (Qualified / Stretch / Unqualified) \- 3 strongest matching qualification areas   
2\. GAP ANALYSIS MATRIX \- Categorize gaps into:   
a) Technical Hard Requirements (e.g., Enterprise Hardware, Routing Protocols)   
b) Preferred Qualifications & Certifications   
c) Title / Experience Trajectory Gaps \- For each gap, specify how to bridge it via Home Labs, Certifications, or Resume Framing.   
3\. RESUME BULLET REFACTORING \- Select 3 bullet points from Candidate Background and rewrite them in the Google X-Y-Z format (Accomplished \[X\], measured by \[Y\], by doing \[Z\]) to align directly with \[JOB\_TITLE\] keywords.   
4\. TECHNICAL INTERVIEW PREP \- List 3 technical scenario questions the interviewer is most likely to ask regarding the candidate's weakest areas, along with model response outlines.”

### What the Al helped me understand

The AI coach helped me understand my level of qualification and what gaps I face. It highlighted my IT support experience and baseline qualifications that make me a qualified candidate. Additionally, it highlighted my gaps, and what exact skills are missing from my resume.

## Example 3: Career Action Plan

### My prompt 

“Help me design a 30-day timeline to address my technical skill gaps. Before you output the timeline, you must think step-by-step. Show your reasoning explicitly using three sequential steps: 

* Step 1: System Baseline Assessment identify what I'm missing   
* Step 2: Resource Allocation map uploaded documentation to those gaps

## 5\. Testing and Improving My Assistant

### Original prompt

“Am I qualified for the Jr network admin position at Boot barn”

### Improved prompt

\[CONTEXT\] The candidate is a CS graduate pivoting from IT internship and general Systems Support into a specialized networking role. You must evaluate work history, academic background, and hands-on home labs/projects.

a) Technical Hard Requirements (e.g., Enterprise Hardware, Routing Protocols)   
b) Preferred Qualifications & Certifications

3\. RESUME BULLET REFACTORING \- Select 3 bullet points from Candidate Background and rewrite them in the Google X-Y-Z format (Accomplished \[X\], measured by \[Y\], by doing \[Z\]) to align directly with \[JOB\_TITLE\] keywords.   
4\. TECHNICAL INTERVIEW PREP \- List 3 technical scenario questions the interviewer is most likely to ask regarding the candidate's weakest areas, along with model response outlines.”

### What changed and why

When working on the career guidance section, I first prompted the coach using a very generic prompt, which produced an output that lacked a true understanding of the problem’ s context. I practiced refactoring my prompt by being more descriptive, including a role for the coach, context from which to follow, variables, expected output, and constraints. The produced output was a far more structured response, and gave me a much clearer image of my qualifications and gaps.

# 6\. Responsible Al

To check for accuracy from the assistant, first an understanding of the source materials and overall objective is required. Diligence should always be in the mind of the user when working with AI. It is the responsibility of the user to:

* Verify facts  
* Check biases  
* Ensure accuracy  
* Check for usage rights

PII should not be shared with the AI. For purposes of this tool, only general information about the user's technical qualifications are shared.

2\. What was one limitation or mistake I discovered?

4\. What would I improve in a future version?

The most useful output produced by the AI coach is the roadmap. This roadmap is a result of understanding my current qualifications, as well as a clear goal in the form of a job description. It creates a clear image of what gaps to address, and supports a growth mindset.

Limitations I encountered correlate to my ability to be descriptive in my prompts and discern output. In the beginning, I encountered hallucinations in the form of my AI coach guessing my qualifications and desires. This had to be addressed in the refactored prompt.

This assistant can be an invaluable resource to someone aiming to achieve a goal, but not being certain how to start. It can evaluate current progression, and suggest tasks that are relevant to the completion of the goal.
